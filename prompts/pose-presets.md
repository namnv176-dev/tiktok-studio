# Dáng khoe trang phục

Mỗi mục `## <tên>` là một câu POSE bằng tiếng Anh, thay vào đoạn `POSE:` trong `prompts/cloth-change.md`. Muốn biết nền nào hợp dáng nào thì xem dòng "Dáng hợp" của từng cảnh trong `background-checkin.md` và `background-cafe-hien-dai.md`.

## Nguyên tắc (nghiên cứu 04/11/2026)
- Thấy **trọn bộ** từ đầu tới chân. Máy đặt ngang ngực hoặc hơi thấp hơn để kéo dài chân.
- **Đứng sát một bức tường trơn**: trắng, xám nhám, be, đá khối, góc tường hai màu, gờ tường thấp hoặc mặt tiền đá kem. Mép ảnh có chậu cây hoặc cành lá. Không đứng trước không gian rộng. Đây là điều thấy ở gần như mọi ảnh minh họa "sang chảnh".
- **Nghiêng người 30–45°, dáng chữ S**: dồn trọng tâm vào chân sau, hông đẩy nhẹ, lưng thẳng, mở vai. Không đứng thẳng mặt máy, không đặt hai chân thẳng hàng.
- **Cho vải chuyển động**: bước đi, xoay người, nhấc nhẹ chân váy. Quay lưng rồi ngoảnh lại để khoe phần lưng. Chéo chân hoặc tựa tường để khoe giày.
- **Tay có việc**: cầm túi (hai tay trước đùi, treo khuỷu tay, xách quai), chạm tóc, chạm tường, khoanh tay với đồ công sở.
- **Ánh mắt tự nhiên**: nhìn xuống túi, nhìn xa, ngẩng lên, cười nhẹ. Tránh cười cứng.

Nguồn: toplist.vn (10 cách tạo dáng chụp ảnh đứng cho nữ), ngoisao.vn (6 kiểu tạo dáng chụp ảnh đường phố), fptshop.com.vn (cách tạo dáng chụp ảnh cho nữ), vinwonders.com (10 nguyên tắc tạo dáng), mia.vn (tạo dáng với túi xách), canon-asia.com (kỹ thuật tạo dáng chân dung), 163.com (高级感 背景), Shotkit, Facetune, Who What Wear.

# Selfie qua gương

## mirror-classic
POSE: a full-body mirror selfie. She stands facing the mirror in the center of the frame, one hand holding a white smartphone up beside her face at eye level, the other arm relaxed down with fingers lightly touching the side of the outfit, legs together with one knee slightly bent, head slightly tilted, soft natural smile.

## mirror-side-angle
POSE: a full-body mirror selfie with her body turned about 40 degrees to the side to show the silhouette and waistline. Weight on the back leg, front knee bent and crossing slightly in front, one hand holding a white smartphone at chest height, the other hand resting lightly on her waist. Chin slightly down, eyes looking at the phone screen, relaxed shoulders.

## mirror-phone-face
POSE: a full-body mirror selfie where the white smartphone held at face height covers part of her face but her eyes, hair and jawline stay visible. Standing straight, one hip slightly popped to the side, free hand lightly holding the strap of a small handbag at her side, feet in a narrow stance with one toe pointed forward to lengthen the leg.

# Đứng sát tường

## lean-wall
POSE: leaning one shoulder lightly against the wall, body angled about 30 degrees to the camera, ankles crossed, both hands holding a small handbag in front of her thighs, chin slightly lifted, gazing into the distance off-camera.

## lean-back-wall
POSE: leaning her back and shoulders lightly against the wall, one leg straight and the other knee bent with that foot crossed in front of the standing ankle, so the legs look long. One hand holds a small handbag at her side, the other hangs relaxed, head turned slightly to the side, gazing into the distance off-camera.

## hand-on-wall
POSE: standing sideways beside the wall, one arm raised with the palm resting lightly on the wall above shoulder height, the other hand holding a small handbag by the strap at her hip. Weight on the leg closest to the wall, the other leg stepping slightly forward, looking softly at the camera.

## s-curve-bag
POSE: standing with her body angled about 45 degrees to the camera in a soft S-curve, all her weight on the back leg, hip gently pushed to the side, front knee relaxed, back straight and shoulders open. Both hands hold a small handbag low in front of her thighs, chin slightly down, eyes looking down at the bag with a calm expression.

## look-up-profile
POSE: standing straight in three-quarter profile close to the wall, one hand holding a small handbag at her side, the other hand lightly touching the strap at her shoulder, chin lifted, looking up and away from the camera, relaxed and dreamy.

## hand-in-hair
POSE: standing three-quarter to the camera, weight on the back leg, front knee bent, one hand running through her hair near the temple, the other hand resting on her waist, eyes looking down and to the side, gentle smile.

## arms-crossed-confident
POSE: standing facing the camera at a slight angle, arms loosely crossed at the chest, weight on one leg, the other foot slightly forward, shoulders relaxed, a confident soft smile, looking straight at the camera. Good for blazers, shirts and office outfits.

# Chuyển động

## walk-toward
POSE: walking naturally toward the camera mid-stride, front foot stepping forward, the outfit moving gently with the step, arms swinging softly, one hand lightly holding a small handbag. Looking slightly to the side past the camera with a soft smile, candid as if caught on the move.

## over-shoulder
POSE: standing with her back mostly turned to the camera to show the back of the outfit, then looking back over her shoulder toward the camera. One hand lightly touching her hair, weight on one leg, the other knee slightly bent, relaxed and elegant.

# Ngồi

## sit-ledge-look-down
POSE: sitting sideways on the low ledge against the wall, back straight, knees together and angled diagonally, feet on the ground and fully visible. Both hands rest on a small handbag on her lap, head tilted down, eyes looking at the bag, hair falling forward naturally.

## seated-chair
POSE: sitting sideways on the armchair with legs crossed at the knee and angled diagonally toward the camera so the full length of the legs and the shoes are visible, back straight, one forearm resting on the armrest, the other hand on her knee, looking softly at the camera.
